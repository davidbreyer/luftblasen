How to Play

Hit the target exactly.

Pop numbered bubbles until Current equals Target. Going over costs a life.

Watch the timer.

The timer bar turns gold, then red, when time is running out. More time left means a bigger score bonus. If it empties, you lose a life.

Every pop adds time.

Regular bubbles add a little time. Bonus bubbles add more. Harder difficulty gives less time back.

Multipliers are power-ups.

Multiplier bubbles do not add to your sum. They boost the next three exact matches and add the most timer time.

Keep five lives alive.

Score as many rounds as you can before the hearts run out.
